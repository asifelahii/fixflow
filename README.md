# FixFlow

> A repair service operations and customer-tracking platform built to make device repair workflows traceable, organized, and customer-friendly.

FixFlow helps repair businesses manage customers, devices, service tickets, repair progress, intake evidence, technician assignment, and customer-facing status tracking from one structured workflow.

Instead of relying on scattered spreadsheets, handwritten notes, messaging threads, or repeated phone calls for repair updates, FixFlow keeps each repair connected to the correct customer and device while giving customers a secure way to check progress without contacting the shop.

---

## Product Overview

Repair businesses often need to answer the same operational questions repeatedly:

- Which device belongs to which customer?
- What issue was originally reported?
- What condition was the device in when it arrived?
- Who is currently handling the repair?
- What stage is the repair in?
- What changed since the previous update?
- What information is safe to show the customer?
- Can the customer check progress without calling the shop?
- Can previous repair activity be preserved instead of overwritten?

FixFlow is designed around those real-world workflow problems.

The current MVP provides an end-to-end repair tracking flow:

```text
Customer
   ↓
Device
   ↓
Repair Ticket
   ↓
Intake Condition + Evidence
   ↓
Technician Assignment
   ↓
Repair Status Changes
   ↓
Status History
   ↓
Secure Customer Tracking
